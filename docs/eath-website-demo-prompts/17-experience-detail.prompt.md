# 17 — Experience detail template

## Read first

Master, 03, 04, 16 and route/catalog/content/asset references. Implements P07 for all six categories.

## Page sequence

1. Breadcrumb Home / Experiences / Category.
2. Hero with H1 and relevant image.
3. What this sample experience emphasizes.
4. Preference cues: why someone might enjoy it, without health claims.
5. Matching trek cards.
6. Associated regions and sample months.
7. Preparation questions.
8. Related sample articles.
9. FAQs.
10. Plan This Experience CTA.

## Data and design

Use experience ID relationships, not text matching by title. Generate distinct editorial sample content for each category: scenery emphasizes views; culture thoughtful questions; quiet-trails a preference not crowd forecast; short-treks available-days questions; photography time/pace; iconic-routes familiar names and comparison.

Use one editorial split, one product grid and concise supporting prose. Do not wrap ten sections in cards. Region links derive from matching treks; month list is a labeled aggregate, not proof that all trips operate in every displayed month. Render all six slugs with a shared template but unique sample copy.

## Actions

View/Compare a matching trek. View All Matching Treks opens listing with experience ID. Plan action → mode=discover, experience ID, source=experience. Preserve a saved planner draft until the visitor explicitly accepts new context. Article routes use fixtures.

## Validation and acceptance

Unknown slug404. No matching treks → explanation + custom request, never invented records. Test category relations, region/month derivations, CTA preselection, compare IDs, unique metadata and keyboard/mobile flow. Do not imply a photo-interest match is a safety recommendation. Stop after this template family works.
