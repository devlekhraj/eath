## Writing boundaries

This is an interface/content-structure demonstration, not verified company policy, medical advice or emergency instructions. Use question-led copy: `Before a real trip, confirm who provides support and what the actual itinerary includes.` Do not invent rescue capability, oxygen equipment, training credentials, emergency numbers, guaranteed evacuation, insurance coverage, medication or altitude thresholds.

No high-stakes practical medical content is required for this demo. Mark operational details as awaiting verified editorial input. A sample heading can show where information belongs without asserting that EATH already provides it.

## Design and actions

Editorial split hero, readable headings, concise lists, one related-content block; no wall of warning-colored cards. Primary contact CTA is demo contact, not an emergency line. Link relevant preparation article, FAQ and planner. No map or external helpline integration.

## Acceptance

Every apparent operational claim is verified or explicitly proposed/unknown; no absolute safety promise; fixture-only copy; accessible hierarchy, demo disclosure, responsive layout and local contact simulation. Final demo can be substantive while intentionally omitting real medical/operating instructions.
