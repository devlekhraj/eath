## Content/design

Write original sample copy about the kinds of commitments a responsible operator would need to explain, not claims that EATH has already fulfilled them. No made-up wages, charity donations, carbon-neutral badges, offset calculations, conservation partnerships, certifications or impact counters.

Use three editorial theme sections with imagery/whitespace, then shorter readable subsections. Wildlife content here does not add an unsupported wildlife product category to the trek catalog. Initiatives can say `Verified initiative details are not supplied in this demo` rather than inventing a program.

## Connections

About, safety, culture article, guides and planner are real preview links. Planning CTA can carry no particular trek. Contact opens simulated contact. No donation/purchase action, external badge verifier or newsletter subscription.

## QA

Distinct useful headings/copy, explicit proposed status, no factual-looking unsupported achievements, no shadowed icon-card grid, mobile reading width, complete links and keyboard navigation. Shared fixtures, not database or third-party content.
